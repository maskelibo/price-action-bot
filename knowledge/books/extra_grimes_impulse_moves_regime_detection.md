## Core Concepts

### Understanding Impulse Moves

Adam H Grimes identifies impulse moves as "a sharp move in one direction." He argues this represents a foundational trading concept, stating:

> "If I could pick one trading superpower, it would be a complete understanding of impulse moves and their resolutions."

### Market Behavior After Impulse Moves

Following an impulse move, markets exhibit two primary patterns:

1. **Mean Reversion**: Sharp reversals against the impulse move, suggesting momentum won't continue in the original direction.

2. **Reluctant Pullback**: Minimal retracement with muted mean reversion, which signals "a powerful setup for another move in the direction of the impulse move."

The character of the pullback following an impulse is the key diagnostic for regime identification.

## Real-Time S&P 500 Example (July 2024)

The author documents a live trade setup identified in advance, emphasizing that genuine learning requires observing patterns as they develop and resolve in real markets — not cherry-picked historical examples.

### Pattern Identification

Three consecutive strong selling bars appeared at point A — unusual for an uptrend. This weakness suggested a potential **regime change**. The subsequent bounce showed limited upside momentum, establishing a short entry below bar B.

The trade triggered and quickly achieved its 1R profit target on entry day.

## Regime Change Signals

Key indicators of regime change in this example:
- Consecutive strong bars against the prevailing trend (3+ bars)
- Each pullback against the new impulse showing weak follow-through
- MAC (Moving Average Convergence) spikes atypical for prior trend character
- Momentum divergence visible on sub-daily timeframes

## Learning Framework

Grimes emphasizes:

> "You must put it into practice and see it play out in real time. There's no other way to really learn."

The pattern — impulse move followed by weak counter-reaction = continuation in impulse direction — forms one of the most universally applicable frameworks in discretionary trading.
