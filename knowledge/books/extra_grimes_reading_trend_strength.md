## Overview

Market price patterns reveal trading opportunities. Technical analysis rests on the principle that "patterns in market prices can point to trading opportunities and potential profits," a concept verifiable through quantitative methods.

## The Pullback Pattern

A fundamental pattern in trending markets involves alternating cycles. Markets exhibit:

> "with-trend strength, interspersed with pullbacks or pauses against the trend, that then break into further trend legs."

This simple concept can form the foundation of a complete trading strategy. The key is not just identifying the pattern, but reading the **quality** of the moves that comprise it.

## Assessing Move Character

Beyond identifying patterns objectively, traders can evaluate "the strength and character of the move out of the pullback." While this crosses into subjective analysis, the skill develops relatively quickly — learnable within weeks rather than months.

Key questions when assessing move character:
- Does the move out of the pullback accelerate quickly?
- Does it reach new trend highs/lows without significant hesitation?
- Does it display consistent bar character (similar size, minimal overlapping)?
- Does it show increasing momentum relative to the pullback?

Strong answers = healthy trend. Weak answers = potential regime change.

## Real-World Application

The author illustrates this concept using US Dollar index charts, comparing breakouts from consolidations:

- **Strong trend moves**: Quick advancement to new highs with minimal pauses. Bars show consistent momentum. Buyers maintain control throughout the move.

- **Weaker moves**: Fail rapidly after breaking out of consolidation. Bars overlap significantly. Momentum decelerates quickly. This signals regime changes and shifting market character.

## The Practical Exercise

The author recommends observing **live market action** rather than historical patterns:

> "Watch the character of the move out of the consolidation and compare it against expectations for intact trends."

This disciplined observation over several weeks develops the ability to interpret market behavior with "new eyes." The distinction between trend continuation and regime change becomes intuitive through repetition.

## Summary Framework

| Observation | Interpretation |
|-------------|----------------|
| Sharp, consistent move from pullback | Trend intact, consider entry |
| Slow, overlapping move from pullback | Weakening trend, reduce size |
| Quick reversal after breakout attempt | Regime change, consider opposite direction |
| Pause then resumption with good bars | Normal consolidation within trend |
