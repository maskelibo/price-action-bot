## 1. Hypothesis (pre-registered, falsifiable)

> **H1:** On EUR/USD 4H bars (Dukascopy, 2015–2025), a *mean-reversion* PA bundle — equal-highs/lows liquidity sweep, pin-bar at FX round numbers (00/50 big-figures), and Brooks failed-breakout — entered at session-liquidity windows (London + NY overlap), with **pip-based** stops and 2.0R targets, produces a **per-trade mean R ≥ +0.10** (net of spread+swap) and a **walk-forward OOS Sharpe ≥ 0.4**, beating a label-shuffle null at **p < 0.05**.

The economic rationale is mean-reversion, NOT trend: FX majors have lower return autocorrelation than BTC; institutional/central-bank flow over-extends then reverts, and the three patterns are all *reversion-after-overshoot* mechanisms. This matches the brief's "EUR/USD mean-reversion tendency, ATR ~0.55% daily".

### Null hypothesis (Popper — written FIRST)
> **H0:** These three patterns have **no positive expectancy** on EUR/USD 4H net of honest costs. The SEC7 standalone positives (+0.54/+0.28/+0.13 mR) were artifacts of (a) corrupted yfinance 1D bodies, (b) understated cost (1-pip fee proxy with no spread/swap), and (c) crypto-tuned params on too-few 1D bars. **If H0 holds, the clean 4H Dukascopy run will show mean R ≤ 0 OR OOS Sharpe < 0.4 OR fails the shuffle test.**

### Adversarial collaboration clause (what would make me retract H1)
- If mean R is positive in-sample but the gross→net spread+swap haircut flips it negative → **retract**, the edge was inside the cost.
- If the edge concentrates in <2 of the 3 stress regimes (range/trend) → **retract as regime-fragile**.
- If the round-number variant's edge survives ONLY after I add FX grids that I hand-pick to fit → **retract** (curve-fit red flag).

## 3. Strategy Design (NET)

### 3.1 Patterns (3, all mean-reversion)
1. **equal_highs_sweep** — EQH/EQL liquidity pool (≥2 swing highs within tol·ATR), sweep bar takes the pool then closes back inside, reversal entry. Short on EQH sweep, long on EQL sweep.
2. **pin_bar_round_numbers** — pin bar (body ≤33% range, dominant wick ≥60%) with wick extremity within `round_proximity_atr` of an FX big-figure (x.x000 / x.x050). Fade direction.
3. **brooks_failed_breakout** — N-bar high/low broken with close beyond, then failure (close back inside) within 1–3 bars → trap reversal.

### 3.2 Confluence
- **Mode:** OR-union with per-pattern independent sizing (NOT weighted-sum vote). Each pattern is its own micro-strategy; bundle = portfolio of 3. Rationale: SEC7 showed they are low-correlated (|ρ|<0.11 in the analysis JSON), so a strict AND-confluence would starve trade count. We want diversification, not agreement.
- **Optional confluence BONUS (not requirement):** if a pin-bar fires AND its wick sits within 0.3 ATR of an EQH/EQL pool → confidence tag for later sizing study (phase 2, not in v1).

### 3.3 Forex-specific filters
| Filter | Setting | Why |
|---|---|---|
| ATR floor | `atr_min_pct = 0.0008` (0.08%) | crypto 0.003 kills FX 4H. Floor only removes dead holiday bars. |
| Session window | entries only in **07:00–16:00 UTC** (London open → NY/London overlap) | overlap = tightest spread + real liquidity for reversal follow-through. Asia-session FX 4H bars are thin/whippy. **Filter on bar OPEN ts, lookahead-free.** |
| Round-number grid (FX) | EUR/USD 0.0050, GBP/USD 0.0050, USD/JPY 0.50 | big-figure (50-pip) magnet. **MUST be added to `_DEFAULT_ROUND_GRIDS` — currently missing.** |

| News blackout (v1: OFF, log only) | flag bars within ±2 4H-bars of NFP/FOMC/ECB | phase 2 — record only, do not filter yet (avoid hidden lookahead via calendar). |

### 3.4 Entry / Exit / Risk (pip-based, NOT %)
| Param | equal_highs_sweep | pin_bar_round | brooks_failed_bo |
|---|---|---|---|
| Entry | next-bar OPEN after signal bar close | next-bar OPEN | failure-bar OPEN |

**Why pip floor:** at 1% risk, a 2-pip stop = enormous position; one tick of slippage = multi-R loss. The 8-pip floor (~14× the 0.5–1.5bps spread) keeps R economically meaningful and stops spread from being a large fraction of risk.

## 4. Honest Cost Model (the make-or-break for FX)

Crypto cost (taker 7.5bps + slip 5bps ≈ 12.5bps round-trip, sometimes "+55bps" punitive) is the WRONG model for FX. FX cost has three honest components:

| Component | Model | EUR/USD value |
|---|---|---|
| **Spread (half-spread × 2)** | per-trade fixed in PRICE, applied to entry AND exit (cross the spread both ways). Modeled as `slippage_bps` proxy = round-trip spread in bps of price. | 0.8 pip round-trip ≈ **0.7 bps** typical; stress to **1.5 pip ≈ 1.4 bps** for conservatism. |
| **Slippage** | additional adverse fill beyond spread, esp. on stop exits. | **0.3 bps** extra (FX ECN tight; conservative). |
| **Swap / rollover** | financing carry, charged per night a position is held overnight, **3× on Wednesday** (triple swap). Sign depends on rate differential & direction. | model as **−0.3 bps/night PENALTY regardless of sign** (conservative: assume we always pay). A 4H trade held ~2–4 days = 2–4 nights = −0.6 to −1.2 bps. |
| **Commission (ECN)** | per-lot; for retail spread-only account = 0. | **0** (assume spread-inclusive broker). |

**Engine mapping (honest, no negative cost):**
- `fees = {"taker": 0.0, "maker": 0.0}` — FX retail has no per-side % fee; cost is in spread. **Never use crypto maker rebate (−0.0001) — that would be a negative cost, forbidden.**
- `slippage_bps = spread_roundtrip_bps + extra_slip` = **1.0 bps baseline, 1.7 bps stress** (the engine applies slippage symmetrically entry+exit, which correctly captures crossing the spread both ways).
- **Swap is NOT in the engine** → must be applied as a post-hoc per-trade R-haircut OR a new engine hook. v1 plan: compute `nights_held = ceil((exit_ts - entry_ts)/1d)`, subtract `nights_held × 0.3bps / sl_pct` from realized R per trade in the gather step. Wednesday triple-swap handled by counting Wed-crossing as 3 nights.
- **Sanity invariant:** net mean R MUST be < gross mean R for every pattern. If not, the cost wiring is broken (Feynman check).

**Conservative stance:** baseline 1.0 bps round-trip + always-pay swap. If the edge only survives at 0.5 bps and zero swap, it does not survive.
